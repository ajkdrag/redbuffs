A Vector space $\mathbb{V}$ over a field $\mathbb{F}$ (be it $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$) is a set of vectors together with 2 operations **vector addition**, **scalar multiplication** that satisfy the following for all $u, v, w \in \mathbb{V}$ :

- **Associativity** : $u+(v+w)=(u+v)+w$
- **Commutavity** : $u + v = v + u$
- **Zero vector** : $\exists{0}\in\mathbb{V}$ such that $\forall{v}\in\mathbb{V}:v + 0=v$
- **Inverse** : $\forall{v}\in\mathbb{V}: \exists{(-v)}\in\mathbb{V}$ such that $v+(-v)=0$
- **Compatibility** : $\forall{a,b}\in\mathbb{F} : a(bv) = (ab)v$
- **Identity** : $1v=v$
- **Distributivity** : $\forall{a,b}\in\mathbb{F}: a(u+v)=au + av$ and $(a+b)v = av + bv$
