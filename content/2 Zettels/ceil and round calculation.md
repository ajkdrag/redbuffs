In programming, we often use `ceil` and `round` functions. A way to do this without calling pre-built functions is to use the formula:
$$
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{ceil}&=\frac{a+b-1}{b}\\
\operatorname{round}&=\frac{2a+b}{2b}
\end{align*}
$$
