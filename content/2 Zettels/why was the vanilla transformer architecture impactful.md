> - [[2 Zettels/transformer\|transformer]]

The [[2 Zettels/vanilla transformer architecture overview\|vanilla transformer]] architecture became a milestone because it:

- Demonstrated [[2 Zettels/attention mechanism\|attention mechanism]] could **completely** replace recurrence
- Enabled parallel training of sequence models (reducing training time)
- Scaled better to long sequences than [[RNN\|RNN]]s/[[LSTM\|LSTM]]s
- Became foundational for future architectures like [[BERT\|BERT]] and [[GPT\|GPT]]
- Showed prowess in multimodality as well
